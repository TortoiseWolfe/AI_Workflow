What lit means

  1. Source · the reading comes from the provider itself (a usage report or rate-limit headers), or from a local count checked against the provider's own figure.
  2. Limit · the real ceiling for that window is known, so it gets a needle. A count with no limit stays a plain counter.
  3. Fresh · the instrument shows when it was read, and goes dark once the reading is older than 16 hours, two refresh windows.
  4. Proven · the reading has matched a second source it did not make, at least once, and the check is recorded in the data.
