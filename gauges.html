What lit means

  1. Source · read from the provider itself, or a local count checked against the provider's own figure.
  2. Limit · the real ceiling for the window is known, so the needle shows a percent of it.
  3. Fresh · read within the last 16 hours, two refresh windows.
  4. Proven · matched a second source it did not make, at least once, on record.
